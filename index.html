The Garage

An R&D tree worth planning around

Five departments, twenty-one nodes each. Upgrades don't land the instant you can afford them — they go through engineering, simulation and manufacturing, so your car visibly evolves over a season instead of over an afternoon.

  • Branching paths and genuine specialisation
  • A race engineer who tells you where the next race is won
  • Every upgrade traceable back to the kilometres that paid for it
Training

Every session counts for something

A short recovery jog is never wasted. Workouts feed your driver, your car and your seasonal objectives at once — and an energy system keeps you honest about rest instead of rewarding you for burning out.

  • Automatic import from Apple Health
  • Running, walking and cycling supported
  • Recovery matters as much as the hard days
The Career

A driver who never stops improving

Your car will eventually reach its limits. You won't. Driver progression rewards consistency, variety and long-term commitment — and it unlocks opportunities rather than handing you raw performance.

  • Progression independent from the car
  • Benchmarks and a driver profile from pre-season testing
  • A team with its own personality and advice
Race Day

The payoff for the whole training block

Races convert preparation into competition: finishing position, points, rewards and wear on the car. Read the timeline, the sector breakdown and the standings — then decide what next week's training is really for.

  • Full race timeline and sector breakdown
  • Season standings that build across rounds
  • Results you can replay and understand
